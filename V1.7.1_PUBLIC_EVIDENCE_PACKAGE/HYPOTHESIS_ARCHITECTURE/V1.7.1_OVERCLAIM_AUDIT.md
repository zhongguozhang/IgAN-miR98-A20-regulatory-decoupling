# V1.7 overclaim audit

Audited high-risk phrases in the V1.7 manuscript and supporting files: renal miR-98 deficiency; miR-98 replacement; restores/corrects deficiency; therapeutic efficacy; effective therapy; reverses fibrosis; steroid replacement; clinical benefit; patient-specific; digital-twin prediction; proven mechanism; established miR-98/A20 interaction.

Result: no unqualified prohibited claim retained. The manuscript uses **unresolved**, **cross-disease**, **cross-context**, **conditional**, and explicit gate-stop language. Phrases concerning efficacy, fibrosis reversal, steroid replacement, clinical benefit, patient-specific prediction, and a calibrated digital twin are negated, not asserted.
