# Gate 0 A B C specification

## Gate 0 Renal miR-98-5p actionability
**0A phenotype/localization:** quantify mature miR-98-5p in IgAN glomerular tissue and localize it where feasible. Whole-kidney average alone is insufficient. **0B function:** in primary human mesangial cells under an IgAN-relevant immune-complex stimulus, test mature-miR-98-5p gain with pre-specified BACH1, p65-related activity, IL-6, TNF-alpha, proliferation, and injury readouts. **0C exploratory biogenesis:** if indicated, measure pri-, pre-, and mature miR-98 with LIN28A/B. A negative 0B stops the renal augmentation strategy.

## Gate A Direct A20 interaction
In relevant human renal cells, use miR-98 mimic/inhibitor, TNFAIP3 RNA and protein, wild-type/mutant TNFAIP3 3-prime UTR reporters, and an appropriate rescue. A negative result ends the A20-protection branch.

## Gate B Functional consequence
Under IgAN-relevant stimulation, test A20 gain, loss, and rescue with pre-specified NF-kappaB and MAPK readouts. A negative functional result removes the justification for protection.

## Gate C Selective target protection
Only after positive Gates 0-A-B, compare control, mature miR-98-5p, protector alone, combination, and A20-rescue conditions. The required result is preservation of demonstrated desired miR-98 activity with protection of A20. A negative result ends the design.

No negative result may be reinterpreted merely to preserve the hypothesis.
