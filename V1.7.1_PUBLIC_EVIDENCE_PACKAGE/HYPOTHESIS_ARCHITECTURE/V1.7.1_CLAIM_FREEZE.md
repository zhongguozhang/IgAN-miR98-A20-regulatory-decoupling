# V1.7 central claim freeze

1. miR-98-5p biology appears compartment dependent; peripheral IgAN evidence does not justify indiscriminate systemic augmentation.
2. Cross-disease human mesangial evidence provides a provisional rationale to test, not assume, a beneficial renal miR-98-5p effect.
3. An actionable renal miR-98-5p phenotype in IgAN is unresolved and must be tested first as Gate 0.
4. Potential miR-98-5p suppression of TNFAIP3/A20 remains an unresolved, cross-context-supported renal trade-off requiring Gates A and B.
5. Selective TNFAIP3 target-site protection is justified only if Gates 0, A, and B support the design and Gate C demonstrates selective regulatory decoupling.
