# V1.7 reference quality assurance

Five new references were checked against primary-source metadata and, where openly accessible, primary full text. DOI and PMID were recorded in `05_V17_NEW_REFERENCE_AUDIT.csv`. The Du, Yuan, and Kawahara primary full texts were openly accessible and checked for cell context, interventions, endpoints, and directness. The Liu 2020 primary PubMed record and abstract were checked; the manuscript uses only the abstract-supported PBMC claims. The Viswanathan primary record was checked for Lin28-mediated let-7 processing and is used only at that family-level molecular scope.

No new reference is used to establish an exact IgAN renal miR-98/A20 edge.
