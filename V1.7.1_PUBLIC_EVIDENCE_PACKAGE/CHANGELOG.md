# Changelog

## v1.1.0 - V1.7.1 scientific architecture update

### Added

- Compartment-specific peripheral-versus-renal miR-98-5p rationale.
- Gate 0A, Gate 0B, and Gate 0C architecture.
- Five targeted new literature records and explicit evidence/context distinctions.
- Updated Figure 1 and falsification logic.

### Corrected

- BACH1 to NF-kappaB/p65 evidence grading: direct functional human mesangial cross-context evidence.
- Precision of the Du et al. human mesangial IP15 context.
- Separation of the Viswanathan 2008 and Kawahara 2011 LIN28 evidence.
- Gate C dependency logic: Gates 0B, A, and B justify testing Gate C; a positive Gate C only permits further proof-of-concept development.

### Unchanged

- miR-98-5p to A20 exact IgAN mesangial status remains unresolved.
- A20 functional role under IgAN-relevant stimulation remains unresolved.
- No target-site protector is validated.
- No therapeutic-efficacy, fibrosis-reversal, or clinical-benefit claim is made.

This release is an update to the historical V1.6 public evidence package. It improves causal logic and evidence boundaries; it does not add wet-lab validation or state that prior experimental conclusions were wrong.
