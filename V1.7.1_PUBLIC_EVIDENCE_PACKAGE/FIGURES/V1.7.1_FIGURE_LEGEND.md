Figure 1. Compartment-specific gate architecture for a conditional miR-98-5p/A20 hypothesis. Exact-disease peripheral IgAN PBMC evidence and cross-disease human renal evidence are kept separate. Gate 0 asks whether mature miR-98-5p has an actionable renal phenotype and reproducible desired effect in an IgAN-relevant human renal context. A negative Gate 0 stops the renal augmentation strategy. Gates A-C are sequential tests of miR-98/A20 directness, functional relevance of A20 loss, and selective target-site protection. The figure communicates evidence grading and experimental stop rules; it does not depict therapeutic efficacy or a clinical outcome.
